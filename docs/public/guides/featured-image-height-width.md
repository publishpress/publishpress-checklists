# Featured image height and width (Pro)

This feature requires the Pro version of the PublishPress Checklists plugin.

With the [PublishPress Checklists](https://publishpress.com/links/checklists) plugin, you can [require that all your posts have a Featured image](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/featured-image/).

In the Pro version of the Checklists plugin you can also require that the Featured image has a maximum and minimum height and width. This is very useful to make sure your posts look good inside the theme your site is using.

- Look under the “Featured Image” tab. The screenshot below shows the “Featured image width” and “Feature image height” options.
- You can choose from “Disabled, Recommended, or Required” options.
- Enter your “Min” and “Max” choices in pixels.

![Featured Image Size](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/featured-image-size-74119b4013.webp)

*Featured Image Size*

If you enable these Featured image options, they will be visible when you edit content. If your Featured image box is the wrong size, these tasks will be marked in red in the sidebar:

![Image Checklist](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/image-checklist-e2b0095ff6.webp)

*Image Checklist*

If your Featured image box has an image, this task will be marked in green in the sidebar:

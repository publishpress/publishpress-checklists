# Prohibited Tags or Categories

With the [PublishPress Checklists](https://publishpress.com/links/checklists) plugin, you can require that all your posts **do not** have specific terms such as Tags or Categories. If a post has the chosen term, you can choose to show a warning, or prevent the post from being published.

- Go to the “Tags” or “Categories” tab. The image below shows the Prohibited Categories options.
- You can choose from “Disabled, Recommended, or Required” options.
- You can enter choices for which terms are prohibited.

![Prohibited categories](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/prohibited-categories-7386c9a5d8.webp)

If you enable the prohibited terms option, it will be visible in the “Checklists” area when you edit content. If your post does have the forbidden terms, this task will be marked in red in the sidebar:

![Forbidden Tags and Categories](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/prohibitied-terms-ef93f2cb97.webp)

If your post does not have the forbidden terms, this task will be marked in green in the sidebar:

![forbidden terms in WordPress](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/prohibited-terms-green-a77e055710.webp)

# Publish time should be at a specific time (Pro)

This feature requires the Pro version of the PublishPress Checklists plugin.

With the Pro version of the [PublishPress Checklists](https://publishpress.com/links/checklists) plugin, you can make sure that your WordPress posts are created with a specific Publish time. This ensures that your posts are always made public at the time of day you specify.

If the publish time isn’t correct, you can choose to show a warning, or prevent the post from being published.

- Click the “Publish Date / Time” tab. The screenshot below shows the “Publish time should be at a specific time” option.
- You can choose from “Disabled, Recommended, or Required” options.
- You can set the required time in the “Options” box.

![Publish time should be specific settings](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/specific-time-publish-d3215bdcdb.webp)

*Number Of Words*

If you enable the “Publish time should be at a specific time” option, it will be visible when you edit a post. If your publish date is not correct, this task will be marked in red in the sidebar.

![Publish time should be at a specific time red](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/publish-time-specific-bf0e1bf19f.webp)

If your publish date is correct, this task will be marked in green in the sidebar.

![Publish time should be at a specific time - green](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/publish-time-specific-red-1-3f27ff506b.webp)

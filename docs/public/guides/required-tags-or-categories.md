# Required Tags or Categories

With the [PublishPress Checklists](https://publishpress.com/links/checklists) plugin, you can require that all your posts have specific terms such as Tags or Categories. If a post is missing the correct term, you can choose to show a warning, or prevent the post from being published.

- Go to the “Categories” or “Tags” tab. The image below shows the Required Categories options.
- You can choose from “Disabled, Recommended, or Required” options.
- You can enter choices for which terms are required.

![Required tags and categories](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/required-categories-833b306bed.webp)

If you enable the required terms option, it will be visible in the “Checklists” area when you edit content. If your post does not have the correct terms, this task will be marked in red in the sidebar:

![Required categories](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/required-categories-edit-posts-8dec855698.webp)

If your post does have the correct terms, this task will be marked in green in the sidebar:

![Required categories task passed](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/green-required-categories-beb1e50a30.webp)

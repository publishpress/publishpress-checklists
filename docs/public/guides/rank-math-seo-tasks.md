# Rank Math SEO Tasks (Pro)

This feature requires the Pro version of the PublishPress Checklists plugin.

With the Pro version of the [PublishPress Checklists](https://publishpress.com/links/checklists) plugin, you can require that site’s content meets minimum standards with the Rank Math SEO plugin. If the content doesn’t meet those standards, you can choose to show a warning, or prevent the post from being published.

Many sites require that their content meets Rank Math SEO standards. This helps ensure that their content is optimized for search engines.

The Checklists plugin integrates with the “SEO Score” feature in Rank Math SEO. The screenshot below shows the Rank Math SEO options.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/rank-math-seo-88f071d872.webp)

If you enable the Rank Math option, it will be visible when you edit content. If your post doesn’t meet the minimum requirements, the checklist items will be shown in red, as in this screenshot below. If your post does meet the minimum requirements, the checklist items will be shown in green.

![Rank Math SEO](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-checklists/rank-math-aaea973441.webp)
